Questions

Everything about how rounds, scores and payouts work.

Does it cost anything to play?

No. Entering is a free wallet signature. It proves the wallet is yours and never moves funds or sends a transaction.

Where does the prize come from?

Every trade of the PUMPICS coin pays a creator fee into the treasury. Each hour, a share of the treasury becomes that round's prize. The amount is shown live at the top of the page.

How is profit counted?

For each trader we look at every sell they make during the hour and subtract what they paid for those tokens. Tokens bought but not sold yet don't count. Your #1 pick's profit counts double.

When can I enter, and when do I get paid?

You always enter the next hourly round, and you can change your squad until it starts. A few minutes after the hour ends, the winner is paid in SOL to the wallet that entered. Each payout links to its Solana transaction.

What is a rare pick?

A trader picked by fewer than 5% of squads in the round. Their profit counts ×1.5 for everyone who picked them, so a smart unpopular pick can beat a crowd that all chose the same favourites. The bonus never makes losses bigger. Pick percentages are shown next to every trader.

Do trader costs change?

Yes. Every day we refresh costs from Kolscan's latest leaderboards, so traders on a hot streak cost more and cold ones get cheaper. A squad you already entered keeps its cost.

What if nobody makes a profit, or there's a tie?

If every squad loses money, nobody is paid and the pot rolls into the next round. If squads tie for first, they split the prize equally.

Which wallets work?

Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and other Solana wallets. On a phone, open this site inside your wallet app's browser.
